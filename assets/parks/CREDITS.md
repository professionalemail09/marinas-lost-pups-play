# New modes credits (World Sniff, Flag Fetch, Pack Groups, Numberless)

## Flags (Flag Fetch)
- All flags are drawn procedurally in `src/mode_flagfetch.js` from simple geometric specs (stripes, crosses, circles). No flag image files are bundled.
- National flag designs are official state symbols and are generally treated as public domain. They are shown here as simplified educational depictions.
- Chad was removed from the pool on purpose. Its flag is nearly identical to Romania's, so it can't be guessed fairly.

## Country shapes, names and centroids (World Sniff, Flag Fetch distance hints)
- Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com), public domain.
- Provided via the `world-atlas` package (TopoJSON), ISC licence, © Mike Bostock. The data is already bundled in the game as `MLP_GEO`.

## Dogs
- Silver and Shadow are original vector art in `src/nm_theme.js`, based on the owner's reference photos. No third-party art is used.

## Sounds
- The bark, growl and yip sounds are synthesised at runtime with WebAudio. No sample files are used.
